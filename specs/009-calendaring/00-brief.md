## Goal

A Vision Law user opens the app and can answer "what is due, who owns it, and what changed" for every matter they are authorized to see — and nothing else leaks. On top of the spec 006 matter model, this spec adds four connected systems from the Justice Quest Legal Calendaring blueprint v1.0 (2026-10-04): (1) a shared, matter-scoped calendar of deadlines, events, and tasks; (2) first-class task control with assignments, dependencies, and checklists; (3) a reminder service whose delivery is queued, retried, escalated, and provable from a ledger; and (4) a versioned matter-template and deadline engine that calculates dates from trigger facts, explains every result, and never activates a generated deadline until a human confirms it.

**Standing caveat (blueprint law):** this spec makes the engine dependable; it does not assert that any deadline rule is legally correct. Rule content must be sourced, reviewed, approved, and versioned by qualified domain owners before production use. Demo/seed rule content ships clearly labeled as demonstration only.

## Security classification

- **Actors:** org admin (no automatic matter-content access under ethical-wall mode, O-03), matter owner, internal contributor, reviewer/supervisor, outside counsel (guest principal linked to specific matters/items), read-only auditor, service accounts for sync/API (scoped, no interactive login, key rotation/revocation).
- **Data classes touched:** confidential (matter titles, deadline facts, attendee lists, internal notes — visibility-tiered per item; external surfaces receive only explicitly shared/redacted fields), confidential-adjacent (delivery ledger metadata — recipient, channel, provider reference; never secret message content in error logs), secrets (feed tokens — opaque, revocable, stored hashed/by reference; provider credentials in a secret manager, never in application tables or logs).
- **Allow events (audit):** item created/confirmed/completed, template/rule version published, instantiation confirmed, reminder acknowledged, export performed, guest invited.
- **Denial/sensitive events (audit):** unauthorized access attempts per policy, hard-date edits and overrides (reason required), cancellations/reopens (reason required), guest revocation, feed-token revocation.
- **Leak sentinels:** no confidential matter names/facts in external email bodies (neutral alert + secure link); no sensitive document URLs inside ICS files (application links that recheck authorization at open); no existence leakage via search/counts/export/feed for unauthorized matters (C-01).

## Pre-mortem

- **Likely failure:** rule content treated as legally correct because the engine is correct. Mitigated by the standing caveat, DEMONSTRATION labeling, maker-checker publication (TMP-04), and O-02 naming a content owner before any pack is called production.
- **Likely failure:** silent date mutation — a trigger edit or provider change quietly rewriting confirmed deadlines. Mitigated by the no-silent-mutation invariant: impact preview, approval, supersede-don't-rewrite (C-04, C-16).
- **Likely failure:** duplicate reminders after retries/restarts — the fastest way to make users distrust and mute the system. Mitigated by idempotency keys + ledger-first design (C-12) and fault-injection verdicts (QA-06).
- **Likely failure:** external leak through the "helpful" surfaces — search counts, ICS files, email bodies, feeds. Mitigated by C-01/C-17 verdicts covering every egress path, not just the item page (blueprint SEC-05/SEC-10).
- **Assumption:** 006's matter authorization matrix extends to calendaring objects without a new permission system. Archaeology verifies; O-03 covers the one known gap candidate (ethical wall).
- **Unknown:** holiday/emergency-closure data quality for the chosen first jurisdiction (O-04). Engine verdicts use versioned fixture calendars, so code correctness does not depend on resolving the production source — but the first real rule pack does.

## Non-goals

- Two-way Outlook/Google provider sync, OAuth connections, webhook/cursor machinery, and the sync conflict queue (SYNC-05–08) — deferred to the integrations phase, pending O-01 confirmation.
- Court e-filing, docket ingestion, or automatic deadline extraction from filings.
- SMS/voice/push notifications as shipped channels (adapters only).
- Native mobile apps (responsive web, per CAL-09 and the mobile gate).
- Resource/room scheduling, firm-wide cross-matter calendar analytics/reporting, and natural-language event creation.
- A retention/purge engine of its own (later roadmap phase) — 009 only respects holds set elsewhere.
- Asserting or shipping any deadline rule as legally correct without qualified content-owner approval (O-02).
- GovRAMP certification work (separate compliance track, per blueprint).
