Platform

One pipeline, from paper to proof

01

Ingest & OCR

Bulk uploads are scanned, validated, and read — every page OCR'd with per-page confidence, low-confidence pages flagged for a human instead of silently trusted.

02

Page-anchored store

Documents live as immutable, versioned pages. A fact is never just text — it carries the document, the version, and the page it sits on.

03

A corpus you define

Your codes, statutes, manuals, and records form the corpus the platform answers from — scoped per organization, fenced by permissions.

04

A grounded agent

The agent retrieves from that corpus and cites its pages. What it cannot ground, it marks as a gap — it never fills silence with invention.

05

Drafts a human approves

Generated documents are assembled from your templates and the record, then held at a checkpoint until a person approves the exact version.

06

Audit over everything

Every view, edit, share, and export lands in an append-only audit trail — enforced by the database, not by policy.
